# Regulatory Sources & Statutory Acts Overview - SIH PS-130

---

### Central Statutory Frameworks
1. **Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974**:
   - Section 25: Consent to Establish (CTE) and Consent to Operate (CTO) for industrial wastewater discharge.
2. **Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981**:
   - Section 21: Consent to Establish for emission chimneys, DG sets, and industrial boilers.
3. **Factories Act, 1948**:
   - Section 6: Plan approval and licensing for manufacturing units employing 10+ workers with power.
4. **Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (FSSAI)**:
   - Schedule 2: State/Central licensing for food processing and storage facilities.

---

### State Single-Window Policies (Uttar Pradesh Reference)
1. **Uttar Pradesh Single Window Clearance Act, 2018 (Nivesh Mitra)**:
   - Mandatory statutory timeframes (SLA).
   - Deemed clearance provisions on expiration of 30 days without query.
2. **Uttar Pradesh Fire Prevention and Fire Safety Act, 2005**:
   - Mandating Form-B structural NOC for industrial buildings.
