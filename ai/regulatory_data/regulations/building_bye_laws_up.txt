Section 12: Industrial Building Setbacks and Road Width Requirements
All industrial development plots exceeding 1000 square meters must provide mandatory front setbacks of minimum 9 meters and rear/side setbacks of minimum 6 meters for fire tender access.

Section 15: Ground Coverage and Floor Area Ratio (FAR) for Manufacturing Units
The maximum permissible ground coverage for flatted factories and general manufacturing units is 60%, with maximum Floor Area Ratio (FAR) of 1.5, subject to structural stability certification by a certified structural engineer.
